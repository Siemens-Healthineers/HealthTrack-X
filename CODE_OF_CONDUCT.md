# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community. Everyone participating in this project is expected to help make collaboration productive and inclusive.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions, reviews, and issue threads.
- Assume good intent, but take responsibility for the impact of your words and actions.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and project security boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sustained disruption, publication of private information without permission, or any conduct that makes participation unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this Code of Conduct.

Reports should be sent privately through the maintainer contact designated in the public repository's community settings. Maintainers must configure this contact before publication. Do not publish personal information in public reports.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project-related communication.
